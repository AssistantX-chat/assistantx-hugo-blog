Nobody wants to chat with a machine that feels like a machine. Your WhatsApp AI should:
- Use natural, conversational language
- Remember context from earlier in the conversation
- Handle slang and colloquialisms (yes, including "heita" and "eish")
- Escalate to humans when needed (not just say "I don't understand")

### 3. **Always Learning**
The best AI chatbots get smarter with every conversation. They learn your products, understand your customers' common questions, and improve their responses over time—without you constantly retraining them.
